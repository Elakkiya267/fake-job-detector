## 💡 Pro Tips

### For FREE Courses:
- **Coursera**: Use "Audit" mode to access content free
- **edX**: Most courses free to audit
- **Sololearn**: Completely free
- **freeCodeCamp**: 100% free, no hidden costs

### For PAID Courses:
- **Udemy**: Wait for sales (often 80-90% off)
- **LinkedIn Learning**: 1-month free trial
- **GUVI**: Apply for scholarships
- **Coursera Certificates**: Free to learn, pay for certificate

### For Jobs:
- **Naukri**: Best for Indian job market
- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking + jobs
- **Indeed**: Wide variety of positions
- **Glassdoor**: Research companies before applying
