About JobGuard

Protecting job seekers from fraudulent opportunities with AI technology

🎯 Our Mission

JobGuard is designed to protect job seekers from fraudulent job postings using advanced AI technology. We analyze job descriptions to identify potential scams and provide career guidance to help you find legitimate opportunities.

🔧 How It Works

  • AI Analysis: Machine learning models trained on thousands of job postings
  • Text & Image Support: Analyze both text and image-based job postings
  • Real-time Detection: Instant analysis with detailed scoring
  • Career Guidance: Personalized course and job recommendations

⚠️ Warning Signs We Detect

💰 Upfront payment requests
📧 Personal email domains
⏰ Excessive urgency
📝 Vague descriptions
💸 Unrealistic salaries
✍️ Poor grammar
